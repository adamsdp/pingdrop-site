In one line

Ping Drop is a share extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac that converts photos to PNG on the device and sends them on with AirDrop, Messages, Mail or any other app.

Facts

  • Price: £0.99 in the UK, one purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac (universal purchase).
  • Requires: iOS 17 or later, or macOS 14 or later.
  • Category: Photo & Video.
  • Developer: David Adams, a solo developer.
  • Get it: Ping Drop on the App Store.

What it does

iPhones save photos as HEIC. Many PCs, Android phones, older apps and website upload forms can't open it. With Ping Drop, you choose Share › Ping Drop and the photos are converted to PNG on your device, then sent. On a Mac, the AirDrop window opens with the PNG copies. On iPhone and iPad, you choose where they go: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or any other app.

  • PNG by default, or JPEG in the settings.
  • Choose the image size, and whether to keep metadata and location.
  • Ping Drop Options changes format, size and metadata for a single share on iPhone and iPad.
  • The converted copies are deleted as soon as sharing finishes. Originals are never changed.

Why it was made

The developer kept running into compatibility problems when sharing photos, and uploading them to websites, as HEIC files. Ping Drop fixes that at the moment of sharing.

Privacy

Ping Drop collects no data. Photos are converted on the device and nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer thinks collecting people's data is wrong. Read the privacy policy.
