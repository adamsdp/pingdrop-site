How to convert HEIC to PNG or JPEG on iPhone and Mac

Your photo won't open, or a website won't take it. Here's why, and the free ways to fix it.

Why HEIC photos won't open

iPhones and iPads save photos as HEIC (also called HEIF) because the files are about half the size of a JPEG at the same quality. Apple's devices open them without any fuss. Many Windows PCs, Android phones, older software and website upload forms don't, so the photo fails to open or the upload is rejected.

The fix is to turn the photo into a PNG or a JPEG, which open almost everywhere.

PNG or JPEG?

  • PNG keeps every detail, with no extra compression. The files are much larger than HEIC or JPEG.
  • JPEG files are smaller and are fine for most sharing. Each save loses a little quality.

Free ways to convert on iPhone and iPad

Menu names can differ a little between iOS versions.

Convert a photo you already have

  1. In Photos, select the photo, tap Share, then Save to Files.
  2. In the Files app, touch and hold the photo and choose Quick Actions › Convert Image.
  3. Pick JPEG, PNG or HEIF, and an image size if you want one.

Save future photos as JPEG

Go to Settings › Camera › Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPEG. Photos you've already taken stay as HEIC, and the files are larger.

Convert when you plug into a computer

Under Settings › Photos, set Transfer to Mac or PC to Automatic. Photos are converted when you copy them over a cable.

Free ways to convert on a Mac

Preview

  1. Open the photo in Preview.
  2. Choose File › Export.
  3. Set Format to PNG or JPEG and click Save.

Finder, for several photos at once

Select the photos, Control-click and choose Quick Actions › Convert Image. Pick a format and size.

Photos

Select the photos, then choose File › Export › Export Photos and set the Photo Kind to JPEG or PNG.
