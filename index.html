Send photos as PNG

Share photos with Ping Drop. They're converted to PNG on your device, then sent with AirDrop, Messages or any other app. On a Mac, AirDrop opens straight away.

£0.99. One purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Why? iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many PCs, Android phones, older software and websites can't open. Ping Drop sends a PNG that opens anywhere, without an extra converter app or extra steps.

Why I built it

I kept running into the same frustration: sharing photos, and uploading them to websites, as HEIC files, and hitting compatibility problems. I made Ping Drop so a photo opens wherever it's sent.

Ping Drop doesn't collect any data, because I think collecting people's data is wrong. — David

How it works

  • Mac: select photos, choose Share › Ping Drop, and the AirDrop window opens with PNG copies.
  • iPhone and iPad: tap Share › Ping Drop, then choose where the PNGs go: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or any other app.
  • The converted copies are deleted as soon as sharing finishes. Your originals are never changed.
  • Choose the image size and whether to keep metadata and location in the Ping Drop app.

Private by design

Ping Drop works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics and no internet connection. Read the privacy policy.

One purchase works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Requires iOS 17 or macOS 14 or later.
